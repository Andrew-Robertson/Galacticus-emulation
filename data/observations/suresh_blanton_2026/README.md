# Suresh & Blanton (2026) comparison data

`table1_radiative_agn_fraction_bands.csv` transcribes the lower and upper
1-sigma bounds in Table 1 of version 1 of:

Arjun Suresh & Michael R. Blanton, *AGN Feedback Models and AGN Demographics
II: Comparing Predictions of Radiative and Total Feedback to Observations*,
arXiv:2607.19603v1 (2026), <https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19603v1>.

The values are bounds on `log10(F_AGN)` for the observational radiative-AGN
constraint, split into star-forming and quiescent populations. They are not
midpoint measurements. The observational AGN definition is lambda > 1e-3.
